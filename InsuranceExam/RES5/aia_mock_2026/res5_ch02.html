    Question 002 of 45

    (QID: 5227e47345) Under PART VIII of the FAA–Offences, which of the following is NOT an officer in relation to a body corporate?

    Director. Front desk executive. Member of the committee of management. Secretary.

    Show AnswerExplanation: An officer, in relation to a body corporate, means a director, member of the committee of management, chief executive, manager, secretary, or other similar officer of the body, and includes a person purporting to act in any such capacity. Front desk executives are NOT considered officers.

    Question 003 of 45

    (QID: 5227e47358) Jane is a provisional representative of a licensed FA KLG Limited. She makes a misleading statement to a client John about structured deposits. She tells the client that the returns from the structured deposit are guaranteed. In this context, which of the following statements is TRUE?

    Jane will not be guilty of an offence under the FAA if John ultimately does not invest in the product. Jane is guilty of an offence under the FAA if the statement was made with an intention to deceive John. It is the responsibility of John to research about investment products under the principle of caveat emptor (buyer be aware). KLG is not responsible for Jane’s conduct if it has trained her in structured deposits.

    Show AnswerExplanation: Based on Section 35 of the FAA read together with Section 20(4) and Section 37 of the FAA, licensed financial advisers must not, with intent to deceive, make a false or misleading statement as to any amount that would be payable in respect of a proposed contract in respect of any investment product. Any licensed financial adviser who contravenes Section 26 shall, notwithstanding that a contract does not come into being, be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $50,000, or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 12 months or to both. Therefore, Jane is guilty of an offence. KLG remains responsible for the conduct of its representative.

    Question 004 of 45

The FA must seek prior approval from MAS for appointing Jane as the CEO only if Jane’s family is based in Singapore. (Option 4 missing in original)

    Show AnswerExplanation: FAs must seek prior approval from MAS for the appointment of its CEO or director or when changing the nature of a director from one that is non-executive to one that is executive. The above requirements DO NOT apply to the appointment of a director of a foreign company, or the change in the nature of the appointment of a director (not based in Singapore) of a foreign company and who is not directly responsible for the FA’s business in Singapore.     Question 009 of 45

    (QID: 5227e47360) KLG Limited is a financial adviser with a proprietary trading desk. It invests in various financial instruments from time to time. Which of the following transactions it NEED NOT record in the register of Interests in Listed Specified Products under Regulation 20A(1) of the FAR?

    Sale of listed units of a CIS. Sale of units of a business trust listed on the SGX. Purchase of options contracts on SGX to hedge long positions in index futures contracts. Investment in OTC CFDs related to shares of a SGX-listed company.

    Show AnswerExplanation: Regulation 20A(1) of the FAR, inter-alia, requires the licensed financial adviser and its representatives to maintain a register of his interests in Listed Specified Products and enter in the register, within seven days after the date that he acquires any interest in Listed Specified Products, particulars of the Listed Specified Products in which he has an interest and particulars of his interest in those Listed Specified Products. Changes in the interests in Listed Specified Products must also be recorded. Since OTC CFDs are not listed, the investment need not be entered into the register. Purchase or sale of listed CIS units, shares, and options must be recorded. Section 2(1) of the SFA defines specified products as securities, specified securities-based derivatives contracts or units in a collective investment scheme. The term securities includes shares, units in a business trust or any instrument conferring or representing a legal or beneficial ownership interest in a corporation, partnership or limited liability partnership.

    Question 010 of 45

    (QID: 5227e47359) James promises his client John that he will help him get life insurance with the best premiums and the highest possible sum assured. To reduce the premium, James wrongly states in the proposal form that John is a non-smoker and writes John’s age as 35 years, instead of the real age of 38 years. In this context,

    James can be jailed for these acts. James can be fined for these acts. If the insurer rejects John’s proposal, James will not be guilty of any offence. John is also responsible for misrepresentation if he knowingly signs on the proposal form.

    Show AnswerExplanation: Section 43(2) of the FAA provides that no licensed financial adviser shall, with intent to deceive, in relation to a proposed contract of insurance write on a form, being a form that is given or sent to an insurer, any matter that is material to the contract and is false or misleading in a material particular. Any licensed financial adviser who contravenes this section shall, NOTWITHSTANDING that a contract of insurance does not come into being, be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $25,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 12 months or to both. James intended to deceive and hence will be guilty of an offence. John will also be responsible for the misrepresentation if he knowingly signs the proposal form with the wrong information.
