    Question 009 of 60

    (QID: 5227e48360) Which one of the following best describes a professional's approach to managing self-interest?

    Prioritizing their interests at all costs. Balancing their commercial interests with ethical considerations. Completely ignoring their own interests. Focusing solely on the client's financial gain.

    Show AnswerExplanation: Balancing commercial interests with ethical considerations is correct. A professional's practice must be commercially feasible because he is accountable to his family financially. Therefore, it is reasonable for a professional to impose a profitability factor into his practice while serving the client. But the subtle point is that if there is a conflict of interest between the client's and the professional's interest, the professional is expected to allow the client's interest to prevail within a reasonable scope. The professional is not expected to do free work, and if the client's interest is too overwhelming, the professional should ask to be discharged from serving the client.

    Show AnswerExplanation: Using client funds for personal investments would not be consistent with professional behavior, as it violates the principles of integrity, honesty, and the management of self-interest in favor of the client's interest.
