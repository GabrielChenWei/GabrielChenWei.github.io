    Question 019 of 108

    (QID: 5227e47927) For the purposes of MAS Guideline No FAA-G14 on the Balanced Scorecard Framework, which of the following persons can be considered a selected client?

    John who is a 62 year old man with no knowledge of written English, when investing in products with documentation in English. Jane who is a 65 year old MBA who is proficient in written and spoken English. Roger who is a 62 year old MBA with good knowledge of written and spoken Chinese, but no knowledge of English, when dealing with a Chinese investment product having all product documentation in Chinese recommended by a Chinese representative. Robert who is a 56 year old graduate who is proficient in written and spoken English.

    Show AnswerExplanation: A selected client in relation to a financial adviser means any client of the financial adviser who meets any TWO of the following criteria: He is 62 years of age or older, is not proficient in spoken or written English, and has below GCE O level or N level certifications, or equivalent academic qualifications. The test of English proficiency DOES NOT apply if the representative provided the financial advisory services to the client in a language (other than English) which the representative and the client are proficient in and all sales documentation provided to the client is written in that language.     Show AnswerExplanation: Any client who indicates that he may need to withdraw his funds prior to the maturity of the structured deposit to meet certain needs (such as an elderly person) should be encouraged to seek advice from a financial adviser.
