    Question 003 of 47

    (QID: 5227e54884) Which of the following statements about the definition of 'critical systems' in the Notice On Technology Risk Management (Notice No: FSM-N23) is FALSE?

    It refers to a system that processes transactions that are time critical. It refers to a system that provides essential services to customers. Its failure reduces the efficiency of the routine administrative functions. Its disruption may cause significant disruption to the operations of the FA.

    Show AnswerExplanation: A Critical System, in relation to a licensee, means a system, the failure of which will cause significant disruption to the operations of the licensee or materially impact the licensee's service to its customers.     Question 004 of 47

    (QID: 5227e47616) A licensed financial adviser ABC Ltd. discovers a suspicious incident involving financial impropriety. ABC:

    May choose not to file a report with the MAS but must document the reasons for not doing so. Must file a STR. Must file a police report. Must order an internal audit to understand the cause of the incident.

    Show AnswerExplanation: A licensed FA MUST lodge a report with the MAS IF it discovers suspicious activity that is material to its safety, soundness, or reputation. It may choose not to report the matter to the MAS if, for example, the incident is not material but must document the reasons for not doing so. If ABC decides not to report the incident to the Authority, it must document the reasons for its decision. A STR is not required unless there is suspicion of ML/TF. A police report is generally required for an incident of fraud.

    Question 010 of 47

    (QID: 5227e54888) Which of the following statements about the definitions of 'IT Security Incident' and 'Relevant Incident' in the Notice On Technology Risk Management (Notice No: FSM-N23) is TRUE?

    IT security incidents necessarily involve security breaches. All Relevant Incidents are IT security incidents. A relevant incident may have a minor impact on the licensee's operations. All IT security incidents have a severe and widespread impact on the FA's operations.

    Show AnswerExplanation: An IT security incident means an event that involves a security breach, such as hacking of, intrusion into, or denial of service attack on, a critical system, OR a system which compromises the security, integrity or confidentiality of customer information. A relevant incident means a system malfunction or IT security incident which has a severe and widespread impact on the licensee's operations or materially impacts the licensee's service to its customers. Critical System, in relation to a licensee, means a system, the failure of which will cause significant disruption to the operations of the licensee or materially impact the licensee's service to its customers. Examples include systems which process transactions that are time critical, or provide essential services to customers. Therefore, all relevant incidents are NOT IT security incidents. All relevant incidents SIGNIFICANTLY impact on the licensee's operations. All IT security incidents do not cause widespread impact because all such incidents DO NOT involve critical systems.

    Question 046 of 47

    (QID: 5227e58343) MAS Notice No. FSM-N24 sets out cyber security requirements on securing administrative accounts. Which of the following is NOT what FSM-N24 covers regarding an 'administrative account'?

    Database Software application A security appliance A Digital Password Vault

    Show AnswerExplanation: "administrative account", means any user account, that has full privileges and unrestricted access to any one or more of the following systems: (a) an operating system; (b) a database; (c) an application; (d) a security appliance; or (e) a network device. The Notice covers accounts that have power over the infrastructure, not tools that are used to manage those accounts, so a password vault don't qualify.
