    Question 031 of 111

    (QID: 5227e47464) Jane, an appointed representative of financial adviser KLG Limited, recommends her client John to switch from CIS A to CIS B. In this context, which of the following statements is TRUE?

    If CIS B provides better returns than CIS A, the switch will not be considered as detrimental to the client. If CIS B has higher costs than CIS A, and there are no apparent benefits of the switch, the switch may be considered as detrimental to the client. If the assets under management of CIS B are less than that of CIS A, the switch will be considered as detrimental to the client. If CIS B's investment portfolio is different from that of CIS A, the switch will be considered as detrimental to the client.

    Show AnswerExplanation: Section 25(6) of the FAA defines a designated investment product as a unit in a collective investment scheme, a life policy (including a group life policy), or such other investment product as the Authority may prescribe. A switch can be considered detrimental by the MAS based on factors such as whether the client suffers any penalty for terminating the original product, whether the client will incur any transaction cost WITHOUT gaining any real benefit from such a switch, whether the replacement product confers a lower level of benefit at a higher cost or same cost to the client, or the same level of benefit at a higher cost, and whether the replacement product is less suitable for the client. Therefore, fund size, investment portfolio, and fund returns are NOT considered for determining whether the switch is detrimental.

    Question 032 of 111

    (QID: 5227e47466) Which of the following statements about dual-currency investment (DCIs) is TRUE?

    The deposit-taking institution determines whether to pay the interest and principal sum due on the DCI in the base currency or the alternate currency on the maturity date. Strike rate means the exchange rate at which the principal sum due on a DCI may be converted into the alternate currency at maturity. DCI products normally guarantee capital preservation. A merchant bank licensed under the Banking Act cannot issue DCI products.

    Show AnswerExplanation: Strike rate means the exchange rate at which the principal sum due on a DCI is converted into the alternate currency if the principal sum is to be repaid in the alternate currency at maturity. Maturity date means the date on which the principal sum due on a dual currency investment is repaid. RATE-FIXING date is the date on which a deposit-taking institution that offered a DCI determines whether to pay the interest and principal sum due on the DCI in the base currency or the alternate currency. DCIs DO NOT guarantee capital preservation. A merchant bank approved as a financial institution under the MAS Act CAN issue DCI products.
