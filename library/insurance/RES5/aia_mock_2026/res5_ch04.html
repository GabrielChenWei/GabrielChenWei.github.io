    Question 021 of 110

    (QID: 5227e47557) An appointed representative Jane committed only category 2 infractions in a calendar quarter. The infractions were found in two sampled cases out of total 10 sampled cases. Each of the two sampled cases containing 2 category 2 infractions each. Which grade will be given to Jane for the quarter based on the Representatives' Grading Table?

    A B C D

    Show AnswerExplanation: If a representative has committed only Category 2 infractions, the financial adviser should calculate the percentage AND the number of cases with infractions in a calendar quarter. If the percentage of cases with Category 2 infractions and the number of cases with Category 2 infractions determined in a calendar quarter correspond to two different balanced scorecard grades under the Representatives' Grading Table respectively, the financial adviser shall assign the BETTER of the two BSC grades to the representative. Based on the percentage (20%), Jane should get the D grade. Based on the number of cases (2), Jane should get grade B. Therefore, Jane will get grade B (the better of the grades). Note that the number of sampled cases containing the infractions is considered in the calculations. The number of infractions in each such sampled case is not relevant for calculating the grade.

    (QID: 5227e47493) ABC Corporation is a financial adviser. ABC's appointed representative Ben wants to appoint his wife Jenny as an introducer for ABC. In this context, which of the following statements is TRUE?
