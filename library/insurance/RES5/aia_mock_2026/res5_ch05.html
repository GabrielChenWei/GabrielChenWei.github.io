    Question 027 of 43

    (QID: 5227e47581) MAS Notice No FAA-N06 defines the term Customer in relation to a financial adviser. Which of the following CAN be considered a customer of a financial adviser PQR based on the definition?

    LMN Limited, the owner of the master policy for a group life insurance arranged by PQR. Jane, an employee of LMN Limited, LMN being the owner of the master policy for a group life insurance for its employees arranged by PQR. OPQ, a trust that approaches PQR for opening an account, but PQR decides not to onboard it. FGH, an SGX-listed company that approaches PQR for opening an account, but PQR decides not to onboard it.

    Show AnswerExplanation: Customer, in relation to a financial adviser, means a person, whether a natural person, legal person or legal arrangement (e.g., a trust), with whom the financial adviser establishes or intends to establish business relations. Therefore, LMN Limited is PQR's customer, the employees of LMN are not PQR's customers. OPQ and FGH are not PQR's customers because it does not even intend to establish business relationships with these entities.

    Question 031 of 43

    (QID: 5227e47576) MAS Notice No FAA-N06 defines the term Business Relations. Which of the following CANNOT be considered a business relation based on the definition?

    Financial Adviser PQR Limited opens a trading account for Jane. Financial Adviser PQR Limited maintains the trading account for LMN Limited. Financial Adviser PQR Limited gives its services brochure to various attendees in an investment conference. Financial Adviser PQR Limited provides investment advice to OPQ trust.

    Show AnswerExplanation: In the context of the notice, the term business relations means the opening or maintenance of an account by the financial adviser in the name of a person, or the provision of financial advice by the financial adviser to a person. The person can be a natural person, legal person or a legal arrangement (e.g., a trust). Therefore, distributing investment service brochures in a conference does not establish any business relationship (according to the definition). Note that a person with whom a financial adviser intends to establish a business relationship may be considered a customer under Notice. However, no business relation is established till an account is opened or maintained, or financial advice is provided.

    Question 032 of 43

    (QID: 5227e47582) John is the trustee of JJJ Trust. John has executive authority to make important decisions regarding all matters of the trust. JJJ Trust approaches financial adviser PQR for opening an investment account. During onboarding, PQR is unable to obtain the unique identification number of John. However, JJJ is an established trust with a good reputation. PQR assesses that the risks of ML/TF by JJJ are low. In this context, which of the following statements is TRUE?

    PQR cannot establish business relationship with JJJ because it is unable to obtain the unique ID of John. PQR can establish a business relationship with JJJ if it can obtain some documents to prove the date of birth and the nationality of John. PQR can establish a business relationship with JJJ provided it obtains the trust deed and relevant documents appointing John as the trustee. PQR must file an STR if John is unable to provide the unique identification document.

    Show AnswerExplanation: John is a connected party in the context of the trust (a legal arrangement). Normally, in the context of a legal arrangement (e.g., a trust) the full name, including any aliases, and unique identification number (such as an identity card number, birth certificate number or passport number) of connected parties must be obtained. However, if the risks of ML/TF are not high, the financial adviser may obtain the date of birth and nationality of the connected party, in lieu of the unique identification number. Therefore, PQR can establish a business relationship with JJJ if it can obtain some documents to prove the date of birth and the nationality of John.

    Question 037 of 43

    (QID: 5227e47580) Jane is a well-reputed wealthy business tycoon settled in Singapore for decades. Jane opens an investment account with financial adviser PQR Limited. John, PQR's appointed representative, handles Jane's account. Jane executes several transactions in her account by sending email instructions. Because of her busy schedule, Jane authorizes James to give trading instructions to John for execution. John is unable to obtain the residential address of James but is able to confirm James' business address, as mentioned in Jane's authorisation letter. In this context, which of the following statements is TRUE?

    John cannot allow James to act on Jane's behalf because his residential address could not be obtained. John may allow James to act on Jane's behalf only if he takes personal responsibility for any ML/TF risks associated with James. John must allow James to act on Jane's behalf because Jane has submitted an authorisation letter to PQR. John may allow James to act on Jane's behalf if Jane is assessed to be of low-risk category from ML/TF perspective.

    Show AnswerExplanation: The FA must identify each natural person who acts or is appointed to act on behalf of the customer by obtaining at least the full name, including any aliases, unique identification number (such as an identity card number, birth certificate number or passport number), residential address, date of birth, nationality of such natural person, and verify the identity of each natural person. However, if the risks of ML/TF are low, instead of the residential address, the business address of such authorised person may be obtained. Therefore, John may allow James to act on Jane's behalf if the ML/TF risks are low because he has obtained the business address of James. Had the risks of ML/TF been high, obtaining the residential address would have been a must.

    Question 041 of 43

    (QID: 5227e47579) An appointed representative John notes that his client Jane has recently made 4 transfers of $1 million each from her account to high-risk jurisdictions such as the Cayman Islands. John escalates the matter and the preliminary investigation indicates a need for filing an STR. The STR is promptly filed. John informs Jane verbally that her transactions are under investigation under the CDSA for ML/TF and she should arrange for a lawyer to prove that she is clean. In this context, which of the following statements is TRUE?

    John has done the right thing by informing Jane because Jane has a right to know about such an investigation. John's conduct is legal because he has simply asked Jane to arrange for a lawyer to defend herself. John has committed the tipping-off offence under the CDSA. This is not a case of tipping-off because the investigation is not being conducted by John or the financial adviser organisation.

    Show AnswerExplanation: Tipping off is the act of sharing information with someone (including the customer) that the customer is suspected of ML/TF or undergoing an investigation for ML/TF. Therefore, informing Jane about the investigation under the CDSA is an example of the tipping-off offence. It does not matter who is conducting the investigation.

    Question 042 of 43

In such a scenario, the financial adviser must not establish business relations with, or undertake a transaction for, the customer. Therefore, PQR must not establish business relations with Jane and should file an STR.
