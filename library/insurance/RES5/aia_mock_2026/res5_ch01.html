    (QID: 5227e47314) Solely based on the given information, which of the following persons can be considered an Accredited Investor (AI) under the SFA?

    John, with _$2 million worth of SGX-listed stocks purchased by taking a loan of _$1 million. Jane, whose net personal assets include her home in Europe (where she stays most of the time) valued conservatively at _$2 million and other personal assets valued at _$500,000.     Show AnswerExplanation: An accredited investor is an individual whose Net personal Assets (NPA) exceed $2 million, but net equity of primary residence is capped at $1 million of the $2 million threshold, OR Net financial assets (NFA) (net of any related liabilities) exceed $1 million.
